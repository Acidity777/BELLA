chapter 07 · the final letter

A letter, just for you.

This is the last little door. Open it when you're ready.

For Bella, with care ♡

Some things are easier to write than to say out loud.

Bella, ❤️

There are things I've wanted to tell you, little things I've struggled to explain, and feelings that sometimes get lost between what I think and what I actually say. I couldn't fit everything into a simple birthday wish, so I left you something here. No rush. No expectations. Just a little piece of my heart, written especially for you.

To the girl I never expected to mean this much to me, Bella. ❤️

Sometimes I wonder if you ever truly understood me.

Not because you didn’t try—you probably deserve an award for patience at this point—and not because I expected you to read my mind. Truth is, there are parts of me that I still struggle to explain, even to myself.

Sometimes I go quiet when I actually have a million things to say. Sometimes I reply in ways that make it seem like I don’t care, when the reality is that I care far more than I know how to express. (I promise I’m working on not sounding like an automated response system when my mind gets crowded). I get lost in my own thoughts, trying to navigate feelings that don’t come with a manual.

And I know how that looks from your side.

You might wonder why someone who says you matter can sometimes act like you’re miles away. You might question whether you actually mean as much to me as I say you do.

If I could give you a temporary pass into my brain for just five minutes, I would. Not so you’d excuse every mistake I make, but so you could see that what shows on the outside rarely tells the full story of what’s happening in here.

I never wanted my own internal chaos to make you question your worth in my life. Not even for a second.

You deserve to feel appreciated. You deserve to feel heard. You deserve to know—without a single doubt—that your presence matters.

I’m learning that caring for someone isn’t just about what you feel quietly in your heart; it’s about what that person actually experiences through your actions. I’m still figuring that out. I’m still figuring myself out.

But Bella, if there’s one thing I never want you to misunderstand, it’s this: just because I don’t always know how to show what I feel, doesn’t mean a single moment we shared was empty.

You walked into my world completely unannounced, and honestly? You completely threw off my schedule. Out of all the people in this world, our paths crossed—and even on my most quiet days, I think about how wild and special that is.

I don’t know what the future holds for us. I don’t know what goes through your mind when you think of me these days, and I won’t pretend to have all the answers.

But I know that meeting you became a massive, unforgettable chapter of my story, and I will never act like it didn't mean everything.

If there’s one thing I wish for you in this new year of your life, it’s that life gives you reasons to smile—especially on the heavy days when nobody knows you’re struggling. I hope you find the kind of peace that lets you rest without having to pretend you’re okay. And please, keep that confidence and those beautiful parts of yourself that make you you (even the parts that used to drive me crazy).

On your birthday, I hope you feel deeply loved—not because of big promises, but because you genuinely deserve kindness, warmth, and every single sweet thing life has to offer.

Happy Birthday, Bella. ❤️

Whatever tomorrow brings, I hope you never forget that you mattered immensely to someone who was still learning how to understand himself.

I love you, Bella. I love you, and I’ll always love you—no matter where life takes us.

And honestly... maybe that’s the part I wish I had learned to say out loud sooner.

Some feelings deserve to be written down, even when they’re difficult to say. ♡
