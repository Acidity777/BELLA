Dear Bella,

Happy birthday, Bella. ♡

I wanted to make you something that felt like you deserved a little time and thought — something you could open, explore, smile at, and maybe come back to whenever you feel like it.

One thing I genuinely appreciate about you is your confidence. And your energy towards me, honestly. It isn't easy getting through to me. I know I can be rude, and I know some of my replies before we became friends probably didn't make it easy for you. You had been noticing me since March, and somehow, around May or June, you found a way for us to actually get to know each other.

That takes a kind of boldness I respect. You didn't have to keep trying to know someone who wasn't exactly making the door easy to open, but you did. And I'm glad you did. I might not always be the best at saying things in the moment, but I do notice your effort, your confidence, and the energy you bring when you're talking to me.

I hope this new year of your life brings you reasons to be proud of yourself, people who meet your effort with their own, beautiful memories, and the kind of happiness that stays even after the birthday candles are gone. I hope you keep being confidently, wonderfully you.

And if this little website makes you smile even once, then it has already done what I hoped it would do. I made it because I wanted to give you something personal — not because it had to be expensive or perfect, but because you matter enough for me to try.

Happy birthday, Bella. Keep your confidence. Keep your energy. Keep being you. And thank you for finding your way into my world. ♡

— from me, with care. ♡
